# Households by age of householder and owner or renter status

Households by age of householder and owner or renter status

## Usage

``` r
GLOSSARY_OF_TENURE_BY_AGE
```

## Format

### GLOSSARY_OF_TENURE_BY_AGE

A data frame with 21 rows and 5 columns

- Group:

  `<chr>` Always "B25007"

- Index:

  `<int>` Each row in the table

- Variable:

  `<chr>` The full name for each estimate variable

- Tenure:

  `<chr>` One of "All," "Owner," or "Renter"

- Lower Age:

  `<int>` One of 15, 25, 35, 45, 55, 60, 75, or 85

- Upper Age:

  `<int>` One of 24, 34, 44, 54, 59, 64, 74, 84, or 999

## Source

https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/groups/B25007.html
